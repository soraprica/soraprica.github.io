FREQUENT QUESTIONS

Discretion without uncertainty.

Can we search without publicly announcing the role?

Yes. Goodmantalks uses targeted, one-to-one outreach rather than public job advertisements. Before beginning, we agree on the information that may be shared with candidates.

Can our company name remain confidential?

During initial outreach, we can withhold your company name when requested. We establish an appropriate disclosure stage so candidates receive the information they need before proceeding.

Do you only recruit executives?

No. Searches can include managers, technical specialists, and experienced professionals in IT, engineering, manufacturing, HR, and finance.

Where do you provide recruiting services?

Goodmantalks is based in the United States and works with clients nationwide.
